## About page

**Header image caption:** "@STARTGLOBAL Sustainability Stage 2024"

**Who are we?**
"Oikos Catalyst is a student-led initiative that supports innovative ideas for a sustainable future. We organize a pitching competition in St. Gallen Switzerland, designed specifically for startups focused on sustainability.
We are part of oikos St.Gallen, a non-profit organization based at the University of St.Gallen. With over 160 active members and 12 unique projects, oikos St.Gallen is the largest group at the university working to promote sustainability and create a positive impact.
Through oikos Catalyst, we aim to connect sustainable startups with the resources and support they need to grow their ideas into real solutions. Our goal is to inspire and empower young entrepreneurs to make a difference in building a more sustainable world."

**Our History**
"The project oikos Catalyst itself is still very young, having launched in November 2023 by HSG students Tom Weltevreden and Bosco Merino. Driven by their passion for environmental issues and social entrepreneurship, Tom and Bosco aim to inspire their peers to engage in meaningful projects that create lasting change. Their commitment to collaboration and knowledge-sharing is setting a solid foundation for oikos Catalyst to thrive."

**Mission**
"We empower the next generation of sustainable innovators by providing a unique opportunity to kickstart their business, offering valuable resources, mentorship from industry experts, and access to a network of like-minded individuals."

**Method**
"We are excited to host a pitching competition at the University of St.Gallen, showcasing some of Switzerland's most innovative startups focused on sustainability. This event provides a platform for entrepreneurs to present their ideas, with insights and expertise shared by leading experts to inspire both participants and attendees.
Each year, we focus on a specific sector within sustainability to ensure our impact is relevant and forward-thinking.
This event highlights the vital role startups play in transforming urban environments. Join us to explore bold ideas and foster collaboration toward building resilient and sustainable cities for the future."
(Note: last paragraph refers to the 2025 "urban" theme — may need updating for 2026's theme.)

**Motivation**
"We strive to support and collaborate with talented individuals in sustainability to create a positive impact. By fostering partnerships and encouraging idea-sharing, we aim to cultivate a community dedicated to exploring sustainable solutions for our planet's challenges. Our commitment is rooted in the belief that collective effort and diverse perspectives drive meaningful change."

## Past Events page

### oikos Catalyst 2025: "Today's Action, Tomorrow's Cities"
"Oikos Catalyst 2025 was the second edition of the new sustainable pitching competition. The event was in collaboration with Student Impact and it took a focus on urban-focused sustainable startups. Here we tackled the challenges of carbon emissions, urban sustainability and circular resource use by supporting innovative startups across energy, construction, mobility and digital solutions. From real-time emission tracking and composting toilets to smart packaging, parking optimization and advanced building materials, these ventures are redefining how cities and industries operate sustainably, driving systemic change toward a cleaner, more resilient future."

Stats: 7 startups pitched · 60 people attended · CHF 1,500 prize money rewarded

### oikos Catalyst 2024: "The Future of Food"
"Oikos Catalyst 2024 was the first edition of the new sustainable pitching competition. The inauguration event was dedicated to revolutionising Switzerland's food sector. We tackled issues like food waste and overconsumption, aiming for a more sustainable future, by supporting the startups in the food sector in receiving funding."

Stats: 4 startups pitched · 60 people attended · CHF 2,500 prize money rewarded

**2024 pitching startups:**
- Marius Semm — Niatsu (Winner)
- Caroline Schmid — GoNiña
- Anna Bünter — Sallea
- Tim Oberhauser — Savori
(One startup joined via Zoom)

**Retrospective by Tom Weltevreden, Project Head '24 & Founder:**
"A rollercoaster of ups and downs, but with a looping in the end! From the exhilaration of securing sponsors, jury members, and getting startups to join our cause, to the humbling lessons learned through failed pitches, delayed responses, and unexpected logistical hiccups (who knew room reservations could be so unpredictable?), every twist and turn has been a test of our determination and passion for this project. But the result that we received in the end made it all worth it."

**2024 event narrative:**
"The event was held in the SQUARE at the University of St.Gallen on the 24th of April 2024. A prime location considering its connection to sustainability and innovation, but of course also because of its amazing view over St.Gallen.
The jury consisted of three distinguished and renowned experts: Karen Gräfensteiner, founder of Gräfensteiner Swiss Real Estate; Philipp Schnöll, Innovation Analyst from Foodward; and Ulf Marquardt, Senior Manager of Partner Development at Nestlé.
Initially, we had five startups ready to pitch, but one had to cancel the night before. Nonetheless, we pushed forward with four startups, one of which joined via Zoom.
Niatsu won the vote of the jury and the audience; Sallea came a close second."
"After the intense competition, the audience got goodie bags filled with sustainable snacks, drinks and coupons from sponsors, plus a small Apéro to close the event."

## FAQ page

**Q: Who do you operate under?**
A: Oikos Catalyst acts under the supervision and assistance of the umbrella association oikos St.Gallen.

**Q: What startups will be present?**
A: The startups of our earliest event are characterized by four aspects:
- An independent legal entity
- Based/created in Switzerland
- Most importantly: a sustainable startup in the specific industry
For more specific information, reach out via email. Startups are selected by the team based on application and fit to the topic.

**Q: How do you choose the partners you work with (i.e., sponsors for prize money)?**
A: We are very selective when choosing our partners. They need to be in line with our values and mission in order to work with us.

**Q: When was oikos Catalyst founded?**
A: Oikos Catalyst was founded on the 1st of November 2023 and had its first event on the 24th of April 2024.
